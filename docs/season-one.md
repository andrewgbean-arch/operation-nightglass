# Operation Nightglass — Season One

Twelve chapters, one a month. Every chapter is a self-contained adventure (five
or six scenes, a few puzzles, one action sequence) that ends on a cliffhanger,
and the best ones end with a laugh as well as a shock. Jack Harrow is the hero
throughout; the running cast grows as we go, and the goose stays.

The spine of the season: Colonel Vasko has not stolen a plane, he has built
one, and he means to sell it. By the midpoint we learn that someone in London
is the buyer. By the end Jack has to stop the sale, the flight and his own
side.

## 1. Vienna (done)
The Nightglass plans vanish from Nevada. Jack crashes Colonel Vasko's birthday
gala at the Karvonian consulate, cracks his safe, takes the microfilm and
escapes across the rooftops. **Ending:** the film is a decoy, Ilse works for
Vasko, and a gunshot.

## 2. Karvograd (done)
Franz fired the shot. Jack follows the real plans by train behind the Iron
Curtain, meets the opera singer Zdenka Novak, charms Auntie Zora in the
station buffet, and finds the plans hidden inside Vasko's portrait in the mail
van. Fight along the train roof. **Ending:** "Tickets, please." Vasko.

## 3. The Iron Arrow (done)
Handcuffed. Ilse's glove, the waiter's jacket, Soviet champagne, three geese
versus Aunt Olga, Anička rescued, the mail van uncoupled and a runaway ride
down the pass. **Ending:** a goose rode the roof ("Colonel"); Madame Novak is
Control's mother; the Nightglass jet flies over. Vasko had already built it.

## 4. Nightglass (next)
Novak drives Ilse and Anička to the border; Jack, Novak's Minox camera and the
goose stay behind. The jet lives inside a hollowed-out mountain above the
village. Jack gets in with the bread delivery, disguised as a baker's boy,
using the village inn where the base's mechanics drink. Inside: the hangar,
the power room, the engineer who was forced to build it (Dr Hana Veselá), and
Vasko's office with the flight plan. **Action:** a night dash across the apron
under sweeping searchlights. **Ending:** Jack hides in the jet's equipment bay
to photograph the cockpit; the doors close, the engines light. "Nightglass
One, cleared for take-off. Destination Istanbul." From the dark bay: honk.

## 5. The Golden Horn
The jet lands on a private strip on the Bosphorus for a demonstration to
buyers. Jack, stowaway and airsick, escapes into Istanbul: the Grand Bazaar, a
carpet dealer who owes Novak a favour, a hammam, a ferry. Vasko's auction is in
a yali on the water. **Action:** a chase across the rooftops of the bazaar.
**Ending:** the winning bid is placed by telephone, from London.

## 6. Masquerade (midpoint)
The buyers meet at a masked ball in a Venetian palazzo. Gondolas, a glassblower
on Murano, a lost mask. Jack identifies the London bidder. **Twist:** it is
Control, Novak's own son. **Humour:** Novak, informed, sighs: "He was always a
difficult boy." **Action:** a gondola chase at night. **Ending:** Control sends
the order that Jack Harrow is a traitor, to be picked up on sight.

## 7. The Wall
Burned by London, Jack goes where nobody would look for him: East Berlin.
Ilse's old network, a Trabant, a punk club, the Stasi archive that holds the
only proof of Control's deal. **Action:** crossing the death strip through an
old tunnel, with the dogs. **Ending:** Franz, waiting on the western side with
a car, says the proof is fake.

## 8. All In
Vasko needs money to finish building the fleet, and he has a system at
baccarat. Monte Carlo: a yacht, a casino, a Grand Prix weekend. Jack must beat
Vasko at the table to wreck the deal. **Action:** a drive down the Grand Prix
circuit in the wrong direction. **Ending:** Vasko loses everything, laughs,
and says he doesn't need the money now: the planes are already flying.

## 9. Thin Air
The Nightglass fleet is hidden in Switzerland in a hangar under a glacier,
paid for through a Zurich bank. Jack as a ski instructor, a bank vault, a
cuckoo clock that is also a detonator. **Action:** a cable car ride along the
cable, outside the cabin. **Ending:** the vault holds Control's real name on
the account. It is Jack's.

## 10. Burn Notice
London in the rain. Jack is framed and hunted by his own service. Breaking into
headquarters, a fish and chip shop that is a safe house, Novak's tea party
with the Minister. **Action:** a chase on the Underground. **Ending:** Control
arrested, and grinning: he was only the middleman. The real buyer never needed
to buy anything. It is Vasko's sister, Aunt Olga.

## 11. White Out
Olga's Arctic base on Svalbard, where the fleet will take off for Moscow on
May Day. Polar bears, a research station, a husky sled, a nuclear-powered
knitting machine. **Action:** a snowmobile race across the ice. **Ending:**
the fleet takes off, with Jack, once again, on board the wrong plane.

## 12. Last Light
Moscow, May Day parade. Olga means to fly Nightglass over Red Square and
blame the West. Jack, Ilse, Novak, Franz, Anička and the goose, the whole
cast, stop it. **Action:** Jack flies the jet. **Ending:** Vasko, in handcuffs,
asks the goose for mercy. The goose declines. The Colonel gets a medal.
Season Two teaser.
